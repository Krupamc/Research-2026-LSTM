South Barnegat Bay Onshore Wind Model Prediction

Southern parts of Barnegat Bay frequently experience sudden and powerful onshore wind gusts during the summer. Onshore winds are winds that blow from the land to ocean. They are the classic beach breeze. (sea breezes) Onshore winds can rapidly change the local climate, cooling the land up to 15° fahrenheit! These gusts are often connected to small, local upwelling events that mixes colder dense bottom-water from the bay or nearby ocean with warmer surface water. Onshore winds can create waves through the process of ekman transport and stimulate upwelling. Upwelling in such a small, localized area has a significant impact on bay temperature, creating a large land-sea temperature difference for more onshore winds, leading to more upwelling.
